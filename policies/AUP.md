# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

1. Do not target systems you do not own or administer under signed approval.
2. Do not use the tool for disruption, extortion, botnet amplification, or anonymized misuse.
3. Public target testing requires signed ownership proof and tenant-level audit retention.
4. All test sessions must be traceable with immutable audit logs.
5. Abuse indicators (high error rate, unexpected backlog, policy alarms) trigger auto-abort by default.
